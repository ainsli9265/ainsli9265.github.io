This live coding engine allows you to specify a melody by entering a space-separated list of notes (A-G#) and rests (_) with stackable length indicators. The octave is set to a default of 4, but can be changed with oct commands. An "oct3", "oct4", or "oct5" changes the octave of all the notes following this indicator. A "-" after a note or rest indicates cutting its length in half (eighth note). A "+" after a note or rest indicates doubling its length (half note). A "." after a note or rest indicates increasing the length by 0.5 (dotted note). Press the button to evaluate your code.

example:
G. C F+ A# D- A D#
oct3 G- A- A#. C- D-- D#- F- G- G- A-- A#- C-- D- D#-- F- G-
oct5 a# c d d# f oct4 a a# g
